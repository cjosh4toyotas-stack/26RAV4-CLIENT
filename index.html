Powertrains

Hybrid or Plug-in Hybrid — pick your powertrain.

The Hybrid never requires external charging — its battery recharges while driving. The Plug-in Hybrid recharges from an outlet or charging station and delivers a rated all-electric driving range; beyond that range it operates as a hybrid.

Standard on Every Trim

Safety isn't an option package. It's standard.

Every 2026 RAV4 — from LE to GR SPORT — includes the all-new Toyota Safety Sense 4.08 at no extra cost. Tap any system below for what it actually does:

Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection (PCS w/PD)

Detects an imminent frontal collision with a vehicle, pedestrian, bicyclist or motorcyclist and is designed to help reduce crash speed and damage — providing alerts and, in certain conditions, automatic braking support.

Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control (DRCC)

Adaptive cruise control that holds your set speed and a preset following distance from the vehicle ahead — down to a complete stop and back up to speed in traffic. New for 2026: Eco DRCC Mode for smoother, more efficient acceleration behavior.

Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist (LDA w/SA)

Reads visible lane markers and, if the vehicle begins to drift without a turn signal, gives a visual and audible alert plus slight corrective steering force.

Lane Tracing Assist (LTA)

Lane-centering assistance that works while Dynamic Radar Cruise Control is engaged — reads lane markers and vehicles ahead to help keep the RAV4 centered in its lane on the highway.

Automatic High Beams (AHB)

Toggles between high and low beams automatically for oncoming and preceding traffic. Operates at speeds above 21 mph.

Road Sign Assist (RSA)

Recognizes certain road signs — such as speed limits, stop and yield — and displays them in the digital gauge cluster so the last sign you passed is always in view.

Proactive Driving Assist (PDA)

At everyday speeds, detects certain objects, pedestrians or curves ahead and provides gentle braking and/or steering support — subtle assistance before a situation ever becomes an emergency.

Drive Recorder — standard equipment on the 2026 RAV4

Integrated drive recording system that uses the front and rear camera systems (front and Panoramic View Monitor cameras where equipped) to record video of the road — a built-in dash cam with no aftermarket hardware, wiring or suction cups.8

Blind Spot Monitor

With Rear Cross-Traffic Alert and Safe Exit Alert, which warns of vehicles or cyclists approaching as a door is opened.

Backup Camera

With dynamic gridlines, standard.

More on higher trims

Front & rear parking assist with automatic braking is standard on all grades except LE. Limited, Woodland & XSE add the 3D Panoramic View Monitor and Traffic Jam Assist5.

Standard on Every Trim

Safety isn't an option package. It's standard.

Every 2026 RAV4 — from LE to GR SPORT — includes the all-new Toyota Safety Sense 4.08 at no extra cost. Tap any system below for what it actually does:

Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection (PCS w/PD)

Detects an imminent frontal collision with a vehicle, pedestrian, bicyclist or motorcyclist and is designed to help reduce crash speed and damage — providing alerts and, in certain conditions, automatic braking support.

Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control (DRCC)

Adaptive cruise control that holds your set speed and a preset following distance from the vehicle ahead — down to a complete stop and back up to speed in traffic. New for 2026: Eco DRCC Mode for smoother, more efficient acceleration behavior.

Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist (LDA w/SA)

Reads visible lane markers and, if the vehicle begins to drift without a turn signal, gives a visual and audible alert plus slight corrective steering force.

Lane Tracing Assist (LTA)

Lane-centering assistance that works while Dynamic Radar Cruise Control is engaged — reads lane markers and vehicles ahead to help keep the RAV4 centered in its lane on the highway.

Automatic High Beams (AHB)

Toggles between high and low beams automatically for oncoming and preceding traffic. Operates at speeds above 21 mph.

Road Sign Assist (RSA)

Recognizes certain road signs — such as speed limits, stop and yield — and displays them in the digital gauge cluster so the last sign you passed is always in view.

Proactive Driving Assist (PDA)

At everyday speeds, detects certain objects, pedestrians or curves ahead and provides gentle braking and/or steering support — subtle assistance before a situation ever becomes an emergency.

Drive Recorder — standard equipment on the 2026 RAV4

Integrated drive recording system that uses the front and rear camera systems (front and Panoramic View Monitor cameras where equipped) to record video of the road — a built-in dash cam with no aftermarket hardware, wiring or suction cups.8

Blind Spot Monitor

With Rear Cross-Traffic Alert and Safe Exit Alert, which warns of vehicles or cyclists approaching as a door is opened.

Backup Camera

With dynamic gridlines, standard.

More on higher trims

Front & rear parking assist with automatic braking is standard on all grades except LE. Limited, Woodland & XSE add the 3D Panoramic View Monitor and Traffic Jam Assist5.

Standard on Every Trim

Safety isn't an option package. It's standard.

Every 2026 RAV4 — from LE to GR SPORT — includes the all-new Toyota Safety Sense 4.08 at no extra cost. Tap any system below for what it actually does:

Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection (PCS w/PD)

Detects an imminent frontal collision with a vehicle, pedestrian, bicyclist or motorcyclist and is designed to help reduce crash speed and damage — providing alerts and, in certain conditions, automatic braking support.

Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control (DRCC)

Adaptive cruise control that holds your set speed and a preset following distance from the vehicle ahead — down to a complete stop and back up to speed in traffic. New for 2026: Eco DRCC Mode for smoother, more efficient acceleration behavior.

Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist (LDA w/SA)

Reads visible lane markers and, if the vehicle begins to drift without a turn signal, gives a visual and audible alert plus slight corrective steering force.

Lane Tracing Assist (LTA)

Lane-centering assistance that works while Dynamic Radar Cruise Control is engaged — reads lane markers and vehicles ahead to help keep the RAV4 centered in its lane on the highway.

Automatic High Beams (AHB)

Toggles between high and low beams automatically for oncoming and preceding traffic. Operates at speeds above 21 mph.

Road Sign Assist (RSA)

Recognizes certain road signs — such as speed limits, stop and yield — and displays them in the digital gauge cluster so the last sign you passed is always in view.

Proactive Driving Assist (PDA)

At everyday speeds, detects certain objects, pedestrians or curves ahead and provides gentle braking and/or steering support — subtle assistance before a situation ever becomes an emergency.

Drive Recorder — standard equipment on the 2026 RAV4

Integrated drive recording system that uses the front and rear camera systems (front and Panoramic View Monitor cameras where equipped) to record video of the road — a built-in dash cam with no aftermarket hardware, wiring or suction cups.8

Blind Spot Monitor

With Rear Cross-Traffic Alert and Safe Exit Alert, which warns of vehicles or cyclists approaching as a door is opened.

Backup Camera

With dynamic gridlines, standard.

Front & rear parking assist with automatic braking is standard on all grades except LE. Limited, Woodland & XSE add the 3D Panoramic View Monitor and Traffic Jam Assist5.
